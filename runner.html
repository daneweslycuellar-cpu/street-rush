HOW TO PLAY

⬅️➡️ Swipe left/right to switch lanes.

⬆️ Swipe up to jump over red-and-white barriers.

⬇️ Swipe down to slide under yellow bars. Swipe down in the air to slam to the ground.

🚚 Trucks can't be jumped — change lanes!

🧲 Magnet pulls in coins. 🛡️ Shield saves you from one crash. 2X doubles coins.

Spend coins in the Shop on longer power-ups and new runners. Your progress saves on this device automatically.

⌨️ Computer: Arrow keys or WASD, Space = jump, P = pause.
