HOW TO PLAY

🕹️ Move: touch and drag on the left side of the screen.

👆 Look around: drag on the right side.

🏃 On foot: JUMP and hold RUN.

🚗 Walk next to any car (parked or driving) and tap ENTER CAR. In a car, push the stick up to drive, down to brake/reverse, left/right to steer. BRAKE = handbrake for drifting, HORN = beep.

🟨 Delivery jobs: step into a yellow beam, then reach the drop-off before time runs out. Faster = more cash.

🟩 Street race: drive into the green beam and hit every checkpoint. Beat your best time!

🪙 40 hidden coins are worth $25 each.

📱 Tap ☰ to open your phone: buy cars and call them to you, see stats, or go back to the start.

💾 Progress saves automatically on this device.

⌨️ Computer: WASD/arrows move, mouse drag looks, Space jump/brake, Shift run, E enter/exit, H horn, M menu.
